# Changelog

## 1.0.4 – November 24, 2026

* improved: confirmed compatibility with WordPress 7.2.

## 1.0.3 – November 17, 2026

* fixed: With Elementor 4.4.0 and Elementor Pro 4.4.0, pages no longer stop loading with a "Group not found" error when another plugin adds its own controls to Site Settings.

## 1.0.2 – November 2, 2026

* fixed: The saved light or dark theme no longer flashes the wrong colors on page load when a delay-JavaScript optimizer such as WP Rocket is active.

## 1.0.1 – October 13, 2026

* fixed: Colors now switch on scroll inside Arts Horizontal Scroll sections with the latest version of that plugin.

## 1.0.0 – September 3, 2026

Initial release.
